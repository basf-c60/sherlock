Sherlock: annotated disassembly

The case

The proof of the sale location needs the burning note examined before it burns to ashes (about 10:24am on Wednesday); the cure is DROP then a plain TAKE (fact 'The burning note...').

The solution, as the game stores it (9965). Say a statement to Inspector Lestrade (SAY TO LESTRADE "MRS BROWN KILLED HERSELF"); if all its evidence holds it is proven and its routine runs, otherwise it waits and is proven later, as soon as the evidence is complete (BC46). Evidence is "seen" (the player has seen both things), "examined" (has examined both), or a marker that only the code named can set. Proven or not, the statements decide the endings (9EA2, 9E85).

MRS BROWN KILLED HERSELF refused without its evidence and accepted with it; the Major's two statements proven together once his confession marker is set; TRICIA FENDER KILLED MRS JONES only ever waiting.

Markers set by shared actions 3 and 22 come from Tricia Fender's and Mrs Jones's own action lists, which are used when that person is the one acting (C933). Tricia Fender is Mrs Jones in disguise. Ask her about the bloodstained clothes while they are in the room (SAY TO TRICIA FENDER "TELL ME ABOUT BLOODSTAINED CLOTHES") and shared action 3 sets 998F and turns her actor-table entry into thing 95, Mrs Jones (E03A): "Oh my god, you found out, I am really Mrs Jones." Now Mrs Jones is an actor, and SAY TO MRS JONES "TELL ME ABOUT YOUR ALIBI" (or WHAT HAPPENED) runs shared action 22 and sets 9993.

What the proofs are for. Proving PHIPPS HAS PLANS and THE SALE LOCATION IS OLD MILL ROAD sends Lestrade to Old Mill Road in the police cab (E7E7). Phipps waits there on Wednesday from 12:10 until about 3:20pm and bolts if you turn up after 3pm; he then makes his way to platform one at Victoria (EE2B), and if you are there with Watson and Lestrade when he walks in, you win. If the statements are not proven, the papers end the game at 10am on Thursday (9EA2). See How the game is won.
