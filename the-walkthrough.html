Sherlock: annotated disassembly

Walkthrough: Monday to the win

Two routes win the game, both from a fresh start by typing commands only. This page follows one of them from Monday 8:02am to the arrest at Victoria on Wednesday 6:43pm: 372 typed lines in 32 stretches, every one listed. In each table, Step is the line number, Type is exactly what is typed, Clock after is the game clock once the command has run, Where is the room you are in (“in disguise” while the china man's disguise is on) and the last column is the game's own reply, shortened. ×N means the line is repeated N times until the reply shows the quoted text. The game is deterministic, so typing these lines from a fresh start gives the same result.

How exact the route is

Typed exactly as listed from a fresh start, these 372 lines win every time. The last column of each table comes from a check: the game was started again from scratch and the route typed in with that one line left out and everything else unchanged. 39 lines still win without it and 333 lose (the game ends another way, or the final scene never comes). “Loses” says only that the exact sequence breaks; it cannot tell a line that matters to the story from one that only keeps the clock and the game’s random numbers in step, because leaving out one line moves every later minute.

  • Lines that can go. Taking out all 39 of the lines that win alone, at once, loses. Taking them out one after another, and keeping each removal only if the route still won, removed 29 lines in all, leaving a 343-line route that still wins from a fresh start. The lines removed were: 2 WAIT, 98 OPEN WOODEN DOOR, 101 OPEN WOODEN DOOR, 111 OPEN WOODEN DOOR, 150 WEAR CHINA MANS DISGUISE, 167 WAIT, 189 OPEN FRONT DOOR, 191 OPEN SITTINGROOM DOOR, 195 TAKE LETTERS, 215 WAIT, 220 PAY 10D TO CABBIE, 236 OPEN TRICIAS FRONT DOOR, 248 SAY TO LESTRADE, 249 SAY TO LESTRADE, 255 SAY TO LESTRADE "THE SALE LOCATION IS OLD MILL ROAD", 268 LOOK, 277 TAKE RIPPED CODED BAKER NOTE, 278 TAKE RIPPED CODED NOTE, 282 OPEN PLAIN DOOR, 287 TAKE TORN CODED NOTE, 306 TAKE NOTE FROM FIREPLACE, 308 EXAMINE CLOSELY BURN NOTE, 309 DROP BURN NOTE, 319 PAY 10D TO CABBIE, 322 TALK TO LESTRADE, 325 HAIL CAB, 328 SAY TO CABBIE "GO TO KINGS CROSS ROAD", 330 PAY 10D TO CABBIE, 370 CLIMB OUT. The other 10 (steps 3, 4, 5, 93, 94, 168, 226, 227, 228, 261) each win alone but lost once the others were gone.
  • Timing is exact. Putting a one-minute INVENTORY in before step 31 or before step 251 still wins; before step 101, 151, 201, 301 or 341 it loses. So a single minute more or less in the middle of the route changes the outcome.
  • Failed commands. A command that fails costs no time by itself, but each failure adds 3 to a count kept for the turn; when the count passes 10 the character gives up and a minute passes. Three failures in a row therefore cost one minute (steps 118-120).
  • What this does not show. It does not say why a particular line is needed. Where the reason is known, it is given in the notes under the stretch; where it is not (the garden loop at steps 52-59), the note says so.
