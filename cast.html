The cast and their scripts

Every character runs a plan script (see CC78 for the language). Hours come from verb 136 ("is it this hour?"), rooms from verb 131 ("is this person in this room?") and moves from verb 128.

The scripts were checked by running the game: over 106 turns every instruction the interpreter fetched was one this decoding finds.

"Starts in" is the room in the file, before the opening minutes are played. "Busy" instructions make the character refuse what the player asks (CBA7). The after-turn routine runs after each of its turns (9C77).
