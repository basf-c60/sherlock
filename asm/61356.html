EFAC: Action record: the second messenger gives a note to Basil (RUN at $EF01)
Addresses named in the title: EF01.
Prints 'A messenger arrives at the door, gives a note to Basil and dissapears down the street.' (7603), with the same output switch setting as EF9C. Run by Phipps's script at EF01 on Wednesday morning when you or the china man are outside, which is why it appears in the winning route.
Output
F NZ; the message printed
EFAC DEFB $00,$00 Action record header: flags $00; byte 1 $00
EFAE LD HL,$7603 Point HL at the message "A MESSENGER ARRIVES AT THE DOOR, GIVES A NOTE TO... (7603)
EFB1 JR $EFA1 Go back to EFA1
