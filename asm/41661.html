A2BD: Gather a verb record's flag nibbles into $A01D and $A01E
Addresses named in the title: $A01D, $A01E.
IX is a verb record (8259): $A01D = the high nibbles of its first two word slots (+1, +3), $A01E those of the last two (+5, +7) - the verb's grammar flags, described at 8259 (direct object, indirect object, preposition before the object...).
