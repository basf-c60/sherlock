9E48: The last scene: Basil arrives at Victoria (the winning ending)
Run by Phipps's script (EF89) when he walks onto platform one at Victoria and finds you there. If Watson (3) and Lestrade (2) are both in your room (9E60), ends the game with 6AF3: 'AS BASIL ENTERS HE SEES YOU AND PULLS A GUN. WITH SUPRISING AGILITY WATSON PUSHES YOU OUT OF THE WAY OF BASILS FIRST SHOT ALLOWING LESTRADE TIME TO PULL HIS GUN AND CAPTURE THE CULPRITS. AS INCREDIBLE AS IT MAY SEEM YOU HAVE SOLVED THIS CASE. YOU TRULY DESERVE THE TITLE OF WORLDS GREATEST DETECTIVE.' - the only winning ending. If either is missing, with 6B79: 'AS BASIL ENTERS HE SEES YOU AND PULLS A GUN. WITH HIS USUAL SKILL HIS FIRST SHOT DISPOSES OF YOU. PERHAPS YOU SHOULD TRY AGAIN.'
With Lestrade left on the platform the same room and minute give 'With his usual skill his first shot disposes of you.' instead (endings/end5_victoria_without_lestrade).
