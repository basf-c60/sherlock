A880: Print the dictionary word that HL refers to
HL points at a 2-byte word reference (low 12 bits: offset into the dictionary; high nibble: flags). Prints nothing when output is switched off ($5C3C = 0: the text belongs to something the player cannot see) or the reference is 0. Otherwise prints a space, finds the word through the per-letter index at 5D80, prints it (A95B) and its suffix. Called by the object-name printers at $A39F-$A3F8 and by $D5E5.
How it works:
1. Take the 12-bit offset and the flag nibble from the two bytes at HL; print nothing if output is off ($5C3C) or the offset is 0.
2. Print a space, then find the letter whose records contain the offset by searching the per-letter index at 5D80.
3. From that letter's first record, spell each record (A95B) until the offset is reached; the spelling is built at $5C42 from its kept letters, new packed letters and suffix.
4. Print the spelling a letter at a time through the word-wrap printer, applying capitals and the flag nibble's rules.
Input
HL A word reference (two bytes)
Output
HL Past the reference
