D99E: The '@' key on an empty line: do the last command again
Reached from the line editor (D8CF) when '@' is typed on an empty line. Unless a typed line still has sentences waiting ($A01B, when the key is ignored), sets D714 and ends the input. The word-list builder at D715 sees D714 and, instead of tokenizing the line again, reuses the tokens already at $5B81 - the previous command's.
