AF6B: Special words and their handlers
Four-byte entries (word reference, handler). AFB3 searches the first twelve for an ordinary sentence, and only the last six - THATS, HE, IT, THEY, BECAUSE, THEREFORE - inside something said to a character ($A024 = 2). HE, IT and THEY appear twice with different handlers, one per search.
