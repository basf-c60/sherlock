DCC1: Default action: take (TAKE, GET, CARRY, TAKE FROM, TAKE OUT OF)
The body is three instructions: the target's container becomes the actor - so carrying something is the same containment link (C86E's field +1) as sitting in a chair or a note being inside a safe.
Input
$A013 The target's record
$A036 The actor
Output
F NZ; the target is now held by the actor
DCC1 DEFB $69,$10,$A2,$DC Action record header: flags $69; bit 5: test D078 first; bit 6: pre-check at DCA2; byte 1 $10
DCC5 LD IX,($A013) IX = the target's own record
DCC9 LD A,($A036) Read $A036 in "Game variables" into A - Its container becomes the actor taking it
DCCC LD (IX+$01),A Store A in byte +1 of the record at IX
DCCF OR $01 Succeed
DCD1 RET Return to the caller
