DDD0: Default action: WAIT UNTIL (verb 50)
Works out how many minutes there are from now until the time typed (the number at $A02F: hour and minute in 6-bit fields, bit 6 of the hour marking am and bit 6 of the minute pm), converting both times to minutes past 12 o'clock (DE48: hours x 60 + minutes, 12 counting as 0) and, when neither am nor pm was typed, taking whichever comes next; the result becomes the time the action takes ($A01F), so the player's next turn simply comes that much later (see the main loop at A064, which also lets ENTER cut the wait short). DDCB-DDCF hold the two times and the am/pm letter.
01am on Monday: WAIT UNTIL 9:00 sets the action time to 59 minutes ($89 $00 at $A02F: bit 7 marks a time); WAIT UNTIL 8 to 719 (8pm, the next 8 o'clock); WAIT UNTIL 8:30PM ($88 $5E, bit 6 of the minutes) to 749; WAIT UNTIL 7AM ($C7 $00, bit 6 of the hour) to 1379, 7am the next day. All four waits are cut short at 8:34 by the news about Lestrade.
How it works:
1. Take the time typed ($A02F) and work out the minutes past twelve for it (DE48) and for the clock now.
2. If am or pm was typed, the target is in that half of the day; otherwise it is in the same half as now, or the next.
3. The difference is how long the action takes ($A01F).
Input
$A02F The time typed
Output
$A01F The minutes to wait
