B6FD: Do the slot's words name some of the room's scenery?
The actor's room record (D33C) has at +7/+8 the address of a list of scenery words - things its description mentions that are not objects (none: Z). The list is read word by word (B77F); adjectives (class $60) and a noun ($50, B772 gives the class) are gathered into a pattern at $AAAC and matched against the slot at HL (A4E0). NZ when they match.
Input
HL The slot's words
Output
F Z if the words do not name some scenery of the room; NZ if they do
