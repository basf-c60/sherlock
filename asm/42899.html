A793: Codes $10, $11 and $13: name someone, then ARE or IS
Names the actor ($10), the direct object ($11) or the person in the next parameter ($13) with A82F, then DE = $007A if it was the player and $0485 if not - ARE and IS - for the interpreter to print: 'you are', 'Watson is'.
Input
$A036 The actor (code $10); $A034 the direct object (code $11); the next parameter (code $13)
Output
DE ARE ($007A) for the player, IS ($0485) otherwise
F NZ for the player; the flags of the compare otherwise
