B98C: Take the command out of an ASK order
The order's verb and objects (+2, +3, +4, resolved by CEDE) become the command; B9FA removes the node.
B9AC-B9F9 belong to the remark path of B936: B9CA hands the remark's remaining words back (node +5/+6) one sentence at a time, freeing what has been used.
Reached from B936 when an actor has been given an order (ASK): the order's verb and objects are turned into the command the actor will run and the order is removed from the list.
The tail from B9AC drops an order's sentences when the order cannot be parsed or an ALL has run out, and B9CA hands back the remark's remaining words one sentence at a time.
