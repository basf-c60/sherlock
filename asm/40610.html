9EA2: Game-over record: the morning papers
Run by Lestrade's script (EAB3). Unless both MRS BROWN KILLED HERSELF (9973) and MRS JONES KILLED TRICIA FENDER (9981) have been proven (bit 1 of their flags), ends with 6A33: 'NOT ONLY HAVE YOU FAILED TO SOLVE THE CRIMES AT HAND BUT THE MORNING PAPERS CARRY A REPORT OF MR BROWNS LOST P...'; if both have, with 6ABD: 'YOU HAVE DONE EXTREMELY WELL TO SOLVE THE CASE AT HAND BUT THE MORNING PAPERS CARRY A REPORT OF MR BROWNS LOST...'.
9E74 is the shared ending: prints the message in HL, waits for a key (C306), and restarts (B0AC).
