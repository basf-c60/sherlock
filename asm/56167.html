DB67: Read an amount of pre-decimal money
'£n' gives pounds; then 'nS' is shillings, 'nD' pence, and 'n/m' shillings and pence ('n/-' for none). The total is worked out in pence - (pounds x 20 + shillings) x 12 + pence - and packed into BC seven bits per byte with bit 7 of both bytes set, which is what tells a sum of money from a time (requirement code 6, PAY TO, wants bit 7 set in the low byte; code 5, WAIT UNTIL, wants it clear).
'£1' reads as $81F0 (240 pence), '2/6' as $809E (30), '3S' as $80A4 (36), '1/-' as $808C (12), and '9:30AM' as $C91E; the game echoes them back as 'You pay £1 to Watson.', '2/6', '3/-' and '1/-'.
