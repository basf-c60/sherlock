E5D8: Verb 129 (scripts only): are two things in the same room?
Refuses if the direct and indirect objects are in different rooms, or are the same thing. 482 tests, equal exactly when the rooms were.
Used by: internal verb 129 (IS WITH: are the direct and indirect objects in the same room?).
Input
$A034, $A035 Two things
Output
F NZ if they are in the same room and are different; otherwise $DC15

E5DC OR $01 Make A non-zero, which clears the zero flag, and hand that back to the caller
