D978: A cursor key on an empty line is a compass move
Key codes $08, $09, $0A and '[' become W, E, S and N - stored in the line, printed, and followed at once by ENTER. GET-KEY (C41F) turns cursor-up ($0B) into '[' and DELETE ($0C) into $08, so on a Spectrum CAPS SHIFT with 5, 6, 7 or 8 (or DELETE) walks west, south, north or east with one key. After OPEN PLAIN DOOR, the single key '[' gave 'N. You go north. You are in a small closet...', $0A gave 'S. You go south.', $08 gave 'W. YOU CANNOT GO WEST.'
