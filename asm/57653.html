E135: Action record: RUN at $E0A7; RUN at $E0E2; RUN at $E0F4; default for verb 20 (EXAMINE CLOSELY)
Addresses named in the title: E0A7, E0E2, E0F4.
EXAMINE CLOSELY. The body, for the player only (actor 0), prints the description (DF5B) and then looks the thing up in E16B, a table of three-byte entries (thing, message address) ended by $FF: the bed (76), the window (102), the bridge (57), the drawer (62) and the corpse (46) each have a longer text of their own.
EXAMINE CLOSELY BED (room 61) -> 'The bed is unmade and one sheet is crumpled up on top. In one corner of the sheet is a very small tear.'; CORPSE (room 88) -> dried blood on the face and 'dark speckled markings that resemble powder burns' on the right hand; BRIDGE (room 88) -> 'a small chip in the stone work that is evidently quite new.
The table at E16B is data (thing, message pairs) that the disassembler listed as code, which is why the last 12 'instructions' were not executed.
