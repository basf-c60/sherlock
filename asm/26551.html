67B7: Message bytecode: everything the game prints
From the end of the dictionary (67B7) up to the verb patterns at 8259, this is text in the message interpreter's bytecode (A59F): a byte with bit 7 set starts a two-byte dictionary reference, $00-$1C are control codes (jump table at A55D), $20-$3F are literal characters, $40-$5F introduce a run of packed 5-bit letters, and $60 and above are handled at A83A.
Two of them collided with message starts and broke the round trip, which is how this was found.
212 messages are split out below: those printed by an LD HL shortly before a printer call, those loaded into HL or DE anywhere in code, those printed or said by plan scripts (PRINT and SAY TO, see CC78), those only a room or thing record points to (room descriptions, for instance), and the rest, found by walking the bytecode from 67FF message by message. The 72 bytes before 67FF (this block) are not an ordinary message.
