DD84: Action record: remember what holds the direct object (RUN at $E476, $E7CF)
Addresses named in the title: E476, E7CF.
Puts the direct object's holder (+1) into plan register 9FD6; the pre-check refuses if nothing holds it ($FF). Used by the follow loops of Watson (E456) and Lestrade (E72F): when the person they follow is in a cab or train, the register holds that vehicle and the next step is DO CLIMB INTO it.
Input
$A013 The direct object's record
Output
$9FD6 Its holder
F NZ; the pre-check refuses if it is held by nothing
DD84 DEFB $6D,$00,$91,$DD Action record header: flags $6D; bit 5: test D078 first; bit 6: pre-check at DD91; byte 1 $00
DD88 LD A,(IX+$01) Read byte +1 of the record at IX into A - The direct object's holder (+1) goes into plan register 9FD6
DD8B LD ($9FD6),A Store A in the plan's verb (9FD6)
DD8E OR $01 Make A non-zero, which clears the zero flag, and hand that back to the caller - Succeed
DD90 RET Return to the caller

DD95 LD A,(IX+$01) Read byte +1 of the record at IX into A
