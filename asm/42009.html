A419: Print an amount of money
HL = a sum in the packed form the parser makes (DB67). Prints it the old way: '£n' if there are pounds, then shillings and pence as 's/d', 's/-' or 'dD'. Divides with A476 and prints digits with A490. 30 pence prints as '2/6', 36 as '3/-', 240 as '£1'.
How it works:
1. Take away the marker bit and convert the packed sum to pence, then print a space.
2. Pounds = pence / 240. If there are any, print the pound sign and the number. Stop if nothing is left; otherwise print a space.
3. Shillings = what is left / 12. If there are none, print the pence and 'D.' (as in '6D.').
4. Otherwise print the shillings, a stroke, and the pence, or '-' for none, as in '2/6' or '1/-'.
Input
HL A sum of money in the parser's packed form
Output
The sum printed as pounds, shillings and pence
