CA9E: Describe the player's room: in full the first time, briefly after
Bit 6 of the room record's first byte marks it as visited: clear, it is set and the room is described in full (D102); set, briefly (D1B2). OPEN PLAIN DOOR, NORTH, SOUTH, NORTH, SOUTH gives the closet's and the sitting room's long texts the first time ('The sun streams in the two broad windows...') and 'You are in a small closet.' / 'You are in Your Room.' the second. (The sitting room's long text came again on the first return: the opening description does not go through here.)
Input
$DD01 The room number
$DCFF Its record
Output
The room described in full the first time, briefly after
CA9E LD A,($DD01) Read DD01 in "Pre-check for moving in a direction (verbs..." into A - A = the room number (DD01); HL = the room's record address (DCFF)
CAA1 LD HL,($DCFF) Read DCFF in "Pre-check for moving in a direction (verbs..." into HL
CAA4 BIT 6,(HL) Test bit 6 of the byte HL points at - Its +0 bit 6 (visited) set: describe it briefly (D1B2)
CAA6 JP NZ,$D1B2 If bit 6 of the byte HL points at is set, jump on to the routine "Describe room A briefly (a room seen before)" (D1B2)
CAA9 SET 6,(HL) Set bit 6 of the byte HL points at - First visit: mark it visited and describe it in full (D102)
CAAB JP $D102 Jump on to the routine "Describe room A in full (the first visit)" (D102)
