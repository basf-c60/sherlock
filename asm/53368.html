D078: Is it too dark for the actor to see? (carry if so)
Only for actors that need light: those with +4 bit 7 in their own record ($A017) - the player, the cabs and cabbies, the trains and a few others; everyone else sees in the dark. For them it is dark when inside something, or when the room's record has bit 7 of its first byte clear (outdoors, it seems) and C00C says it is night (8pm to 6am) - unless a light is about: a thing that also has +4 bit 7, whose +5 flags masked with $5C give $14 (full and on, not a person: the oil lamp starts like this, +5 = $94), and that C821 says is here.
Walking to Baker Street and setting the clock to 9pm, LOOK gets 'SORRY IT IS TOO DARK.' without the oil lamp and the normal description with it.
How it works:
1. Only actors with +4 bit 7 set (the player, and some others) can be in the dark.
2. Follow the actor's containment chain (C86E). A room with its light bit (+0 bit 7) is never dark; nor is anywhere by day (anything but night, C00C).
3. At night in an unlit room, look through all the things for a light: one that is in the actor's reach, alight (+5 passes the test) and shining (+4 bit 7). If one is found it is not dark.
4. Otherwise return with carry set.
Input
$A017 The actor's record
Output
F Carry when it is too dark for the actor to see; NC when not
