E0AD: Action record: Lestrade finds the large rock in the stream (RUN at $E0A2)
Addresses named in the title: E0A2.
The extra step of EXAMINE CLOSELY DEEP STREAM. If the player is in room 88 (the east side of the stream) on Tuesday, the large rock (indirect object, thing 93) is made visible (+5 and +6 bit 7), put in the player's room and held by Inspector Lestrade (the direct object): 'As you search the river Lestrade comes over to help. He stumbles and slid...' (7B5A).
Input
$A034 The direct object
$A015 The indirect object's record
Output
F NZ; the rock revealed in room 88 on Tuesday
