C1F4: Command-window state
The bottom window, where the player's typing is echoed. C1F4: characters left on the line (36 to a line: the font is 7 pixels wide); C1F6/$C1F7: the screen address being written; C1FB: the pixel offset within the byte. C1F5 is the text window's own characters-left count, and C1F8-$C1FA its print position and pixel shift.
C1F4 DEFB $00 Characters left on the line
C1F5 DEFB $00 Line count (see C306)
C1F6 DEFW $0000 Screen address
C1F8 DEFB $00,$00,$00 Not identified
C1FB DEFB $00 Pixel offset
