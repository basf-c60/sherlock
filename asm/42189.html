A4CD: Do the pattern words at IY name the words at HL? ($A4CD, $A4E0)
Addresses named in the title: A4E0.
NZ means no match. A4CD first requires the fourth pair (six bytes on) to match, and is used by the verb lookup ($B3EC); A4E0 by the thing and room searches ($D37B, $D3B8) and the scenery check ($B764).
How it works:
1. A4CD tries the fourth pair of the pattern first, six bytes on; if it fails, the whole match fails.
2. A4E0 then needs the first pair to match (A51B; an empty pattern word matches anything) and notes that in $A02E.
