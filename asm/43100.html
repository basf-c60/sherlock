A85C: Word suffixes
Nine 4-byte entries, zero-padded, chosen by the low nibble of a dictionary word's flags. Code 8 is a delete, so suffix 3 turns SPY into SPIES and suffix 7 RIDE into RIDING. $A93B indexes it.
A85C DEFB $53,$00,$00,$00 Suffix 0: "S"
A860 DEFB $45,$53,$00,$00 Suffix 1: "ES"
A864 DEFB $49,$45,$53,$00 Suffix 2: "IES"
A868 DEFB $08,$49,$45,$53 Suffix 3: "<delete>IES"
A86C DEFB $44,$00,$00,$00 Suffix 4: "D"
A870 DEFB $49,$4E,$47,$00 Suffix 5: "ING"
A874 DEFB $45,$44,$00,$00 Suffix 6: "ED"
A878 DEFB $08,$49,$4E,$47 Suffix 7: "<delete>ING"
