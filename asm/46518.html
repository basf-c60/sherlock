B5B6: Move on to the next sentence of the typed line
$A01B = A; the sentence list 9FF0 becomes what was the rest of the line (9FF2), and 9FF2 the rest after that, the link being cut.
Input
A The value for $A01B
Output
$9FF0 The rest of the typed line as the current sentence
$9FF2 What follows it
IX The new sentence node
B5B6 LD ($A01B),A Remember A in $A01B
B5B9 LD IX,($9FF2) Read 9FF2 in "Game variables" into IX - The rest of the typed line (9FF2) becomes the current sentence (9FF0)
B5BD LD ($9FF0),IX Store IX in words the player has (9FF0)
B5C1 LD L,(IX-$02) Read byte -2 of the record at IX into L - Its link (the two bytes before it) becomes the new rest
B5C4 LD H,(IX-$01) Read byte -1 of the record at IX into H
B5C7 LD ($9FF2),HL Store HL in 9FF2 in "Game variables"
B5CA LD (IX-$02),$00 Set byte -2 of the record at IX to $00 - Cut the sentence's own link
B5CE LD (IX-$01),$00 Set byte -1 of the record at IX to $00
B5D2 RET Return to the caller
