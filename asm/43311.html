A92F: Add a dictionary word's suffix to the spelling at IX
The low nibble of (HL) picks one of the four-letter suffixes at A85C (15 = none), copied to IX and ended with 0.
Input
HL A dictionary record
IX The end of the spelling
Output
IX At the end of the suffix, ended with 0
