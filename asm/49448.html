C128: Flag: text lines are narrow (a picture is showing)
1 while a room's picture is in the top right quarter of the screen and the text is kept to the left half (18 characters to a line); 0 for full-width lines. Set by C129, cleared by C142, read by the character printer (C2E2) and the scroll (C316). Describing the entrance hallway (room 6) sets it and the line length to 18; the next command clears it.
