E488: Action record: where is the person I follow? (RUN at $E45B, $E7B9)
Addresses named in the title: E45B, E7B9.
Puts the direct object's room (+15) into plan register 9FD6. The follow loops of Watson, Lestrade and Straker use it to know where to walk next.
Input
$A013 The direct object's record
Output
$9FD6 The direct object's room
F NZ
E488 DEFB $27,$00 Action record header: flags $27; bit 5: test D078 first; byte 1 $00
E48A LD IX,($A013) Read $A013 in "Game variables" into IX - The direct object's room (+15)...
E48E LD A,(IX+$0F) Read byte +15 of the record at IX into A
E491 LD ($9FD6),A ...goes into plan register 9FD6
E494 OR $01 Make A non-zero, which clears the zero flag, and hand that back to the caller - Succeed
E496 RET Return to the caller
