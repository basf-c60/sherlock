DAC6: Read a token that is not a word: money, a number or punctuation
A '£' (character $60) starts an amount of money (DB67); a digit starts a time or a number (DAEF); a full stop, comma or quote mark gives the punctuation tokens $B0, $A0 and $90.
Input
A The first character of the word
HL The word
Output
A The token class; BC its value; HL past it, for money, a number, full stop, comma or quote
F NZ for any other character

DAC8 JP Z,$DB67 If A equals $60, jump on to the routine "Read an amount of pre-decimal money" (DB67)

DACE JP NC,$DAEF Yes: read a number (DAEF)
