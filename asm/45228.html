B0AC: After a game-over message: get ready to load a saved game
Resets the minute counter and the player's next-turn time to 0 and sets $A0DA = 1, so the main loop (A064) goes to B0D7 at the end of the minute. B0C6 is the other way out: $A0DA = 2 (save, then carry on).
