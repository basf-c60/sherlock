A1C7: Free node IX back to the heap
Puts the block back in the free list in address order and joins it to free neighbours (A20C).
How it works:
1. Save HL and DE, and work out where the freed block starts: three bytes before the node in IX.
2. Walk the list of free blocks from its head at 9FE3. The list is kept in address order, so stop at the first free block that lies above this one, or at the end of the list.
3. Link the block in at that point: the previous link now points at it, and its own link points at the block that came after.
4. Try to join it to the free block that follows it (A20C). If the freed block joined onto the one before it instead, step back to that block and try again to join it to what follows.
Input
IX The node to free
IX is not preserved
