C00C: What part of the day is it? (A = 1-5)
1 night (8pm to 5:59am), 5 dawn (the 6am hour), 4 day (7am to 12:59pm), 3 afternoon (1pm to 6:59pm), 2 dusk (the 7pm hour).
The five parts, from the code: 1 night (8pm to 5:59am), 2 the 7pm hour, 3 afternoon (1pm to 6:59pm), 4 morning (7am to 12:59pm), 5 the 6am hour. The message codes $17, $16, $15, $14 and $0F show text only in parts 1-5 respectively (A68C).
How it works:
1. In the morning (am), 12 midnight to 5:59 is night (1), 6am is dawn (5) and 7 to 11am is morning (4).
2. In the afternoon (pm), 12 noon is morning (4), 1 to 6pm is afternoon (3), 7pm is evening (2) and 8pm onwards is night (1).
