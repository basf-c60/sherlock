C190: The chain strip that holds the clock: a 32-pixel pattern, six lines deep
Drawn once at start-up ($A049 calls C14A): each of the six four-byte rows is repeated eight times across the screen from $5140, one pixel line apart, and the attribute row at $5A40 is set to $78. It is the chain-like strip between the room text and the command window, which the clock is printed into (C1A8) - see the screenshot on the Pictures page.
