E518: Action record: pay everything in the purse (RUN at $E508)
Addresses named in the title: E508.
Prepares the payment step of the sub-script at E506. The direct object becomes the number $FE, and the sum to pay ($A02F) is the whole balance held by the payer's purse (+9/+10) with bit 7 set; the next step, PAY TO (E370), then hands it over. The cabbie's purse balance is negative while a fare is owed, so paying 'everything' from the cabbie's purse pays the change.
Input
IX The purse
Output
$9FD7 $FE
$A02F The amount in the number form
F NZ

E51C LD A,$FE Load A with $FE - The plan's direct object (9FD7) = $FE (a number)
