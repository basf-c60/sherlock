CBA7: Refuse if the character is busy: 'SORRY, I CANNOT DO THAT NOW.'
Called from both E40A (SIT) and E432 (GET OUT) before they do anything else. Checks bit 6 of a flag byte at B934; if clear, returns immediately (continue normally).
"' and he stays in the chair.
