9FC5: Default action: KILL (also BREAK and a profane synonym)
The pre-check is the body itself: it always refuses, handing back 9FCE, which prints message 680B. BREAK LAMP and KILL WATSON both answer 'ONLY AN IDIOT WOULD TRY THAT.'
