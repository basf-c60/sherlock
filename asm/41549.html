A24D: Articles: A / AN / SOME, or THE
Two tables of four word references, chosen between by A7EB: the first (THE, A, AN, SOME) normally, the second (THE, THE, THE, SOME) when $A027 or A2AA is set - so a thing is 'a lamp' or 'the lamp' as the sentence needs. The entry is picked by bits 4-5 of D, the high byte of the article code in a thing's name.
A24D DEFW $089D Normal 0: THE
A24F DEFW $0001 Normal 1: A
A251 DEFW $005A Normal 2: AN
A253 DEFW $080A Normal 3: SOME
A255 DEFW $089D Definite 0: THE
A257 DEFW $089D Definite 1: THE
A259 DEFW $089D Definite 2: THE
A25B DEFW $080A Definite 3: SOME
