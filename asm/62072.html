F278: Plan script: steam train (actor 42) - the Leatherhead line, first stop Kings Cross
Entry point: F278 = steam train (actor 42).
Each sub-block is one instruction. Split off from the block before, which it merely follows in memory.
In an instruction, 'plan dobj' and 'plan iobj' are not fixed objects: they mean the direct and indirect object currently held in the actor's plan registers (bytes +4 and +5 of its actor record), so the same step works on whatever the actor is dealing with - for the player, the objects of the command just typed. 'plan verb' is the verb register (+3), 'self' is the acting character, and '-' means none.
Each DO 140 is internal verb 140: put the train in room n (E4C9) and wait m minutes. Rooms 2, 3 and 4 are 'between stations' - also the rooms a passenger is in while riding. The two Leatherhead trains run the same three-hour circuit half a cycle apart: 60 minutes on the way, platform four at Kings Cross (room 23) for 10, 5 minutes, platform three (22) for 10, 5 minutes, 60 minutes on the way, Leatherhead platform one (54) for 10, 5 minutes, platform two (55) for 10, 5 minutes. So a train leaves each platform every 90 minutes and the run takes about 65. This is what decides the endgame chase: Phipps catches the 4:55, and the next is 90 minutes behind him (see E7E7).
