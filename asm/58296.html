E3B8: Pre-check for getting into a cab
Named in the header of the shared GET INTO action at E3EA. Runs E3F5 first (busy? already there? will you fit?); then, for a moment taking the next number after the cab (its driver) as the actor, asks CFB6, and if that says yes requires the thing at the cab's +3 and the actor to share one of bits 4-6 of +4, refusing (DC15) otherwise.
How it works:
1. Run the checks for sitting on or getting into things (E3F5: not busy, not already in it, will it fit).
2. With the actor temporarily the thing after the cab (its driver), see whether the driver has a step in progress (CFB6).
3. If so, the thing at the cab's +3 and the actor must share one of bits 4 to 6 of +4, or the move is refused.
Input
$A034 The cab
Output
F NZ if the actor may get in; Z or $DC15 if not
