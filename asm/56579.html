DD03: Default action: move in a direction (verbs 1-10)
Shared by all ten direction verbs. The pre-check (DCD2) works out where the actor would end up; the body moves the actor and, when the actor is the one being followed by the display, updates A00A.
The body also stores the destination and its room record at DD01 and DCFF - for ANY actor that moves, not just the player. After the boot sequence they hold room 16 (platform one of Aldergate station), left there by someone moving during the first turns.
Every compass move by every character, including the cook (EAST and WEST between rooms 65 and 68), the gardener (NORTHWEST, SOUTHWEST, NORTHEAST ...) and the player.
How it works:
1. The pre-check (DCD2) finds the exit in the direction (the verb number), and refuses if there is none, the actor is inside something, the destination is 0 or the thing in the way will not let the actor through.
2. The body sets the displayed room if the actor is the one followed, remembers the room entered (DD01) and its record (DCFF).
3.
Input
IX The exit found by the pre-check
Output
F NZ; the actor is in the new room
