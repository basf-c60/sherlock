E5FD: Action record: RUN at $E08F; RUN at $E46B; internal verb 130 (IS HERE: is the direct object with the actor ($C863)?)
Addresses named in the title: E08F, E46B, C863.
Internal verb 130, IS HERE. The header (flags $47) names a pre-check at E604, which is the whole test: where the actor is (C863) and where the direct object is (C863) - each being the place found by climbing out through open containers (C86E), handed back in the alternate A register, hence the EX AF,AF' - must be the same, and the direct object must not be the actor itself (E5F3); otherwise the record refuses (DC15). So a person in a cab IS HERE for someone in the same cab, and Lestrade's 'is the player in the police cab with me' (EA78) works the same way.
