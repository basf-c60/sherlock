F2B2: Plan script: cabbie (actor 31); cabbie (actor 34)
Entry point: F2B2 = cabbie (actor 31); cabbie (actor 34).
Each sub-block is one instruction. Split off from the block before, which it merely follows in memory.
In an instruction, 'plan dobj' and 'plan iobj' are not fixed objects: they mean the direct and indirect object currently held in the actor's plan registers (bytes +4 and +5 of its actor record), so the same step works on whatever the actor is dealing with - for the player, the objects of the command just typed. 'plan verb' is the verb register (+3), 'self' is the acting character, and '-' means none.
The cabbie takes the quotation at the head of the list (9FEC), not necessarily the one just typed (see B01C: a failed SAY leaves its quotation behind), starts the journey, and waits for his fare at the end of it; the fare is 2d plus 2d for each full five minutes, change is given. Two cabs may stand in Parliament Street; if the player boards the one Lestrade is in at the same time, Lestrade's own cab script (EBA4) and the player's ride collide and he stays in the cab for good.
