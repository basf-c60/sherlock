E4C9: Action record: put a train in a room (internal verb 140, RUN at $F2A7)
Addresses named in the title: F2A7, verb 140: F2A5.
The direct object is a room number. The train (the actor) is moved there (DD0E) and the room record at DCFF gets bit 6 cleared. If the room is 2, 3 or 4 (the stretch between stations) 'THE TRAIN PULLS OUT OF THE STATION.' is printed first. In the sampled runs (the first three of each kind) each of the train things 37 to 41 went from room 2 to a room of its own (30, 26, 21, 27 and 31) with its +5 bit 7 set; the timetable's later moves (F278, F28D) were not sampled.
How it works:
1. The direct object is a room number. If it is Lestrade, Watson or Straker (things 2, 3, 4), print 'THE TRAIN PULLS OUT OF THE STATION.' and use the routine that clears the revealed flag (9C60); otherwise the one that sets it (9C56).
2. Run that routine on the train, move the train to the room (DD0E) and clear the visited bit of the room's record.
Input
$A034 The room number
Output
F NZ; the train moved there and the room's visited bit cleared
