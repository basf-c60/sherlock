DC57: Action record: default for verb 28 (GO THROUGH)
GO THROUGH <door>. The characters use it constantly - Chief Constable Straker, the cook, the gardener, Lestrade, the local policeman, Phipps and others walk through doors with it - and the player can too. The verb becomes a GO in the door's direction. In the runs sampled the actor's room (+15) changed to the room on the other side of the door (Lestrade through the solid door, room 94 to 97; Straker through the large oak door, 63 to 64).
Input
IX The exit through the door
Output
The command turned into a movement; F From $DD07
