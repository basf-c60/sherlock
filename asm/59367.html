E7E7: Plan script: inspector lestrade (actor 2)
Entry points: E7E7 = inspector lestrade (actor 2).
Each sub-block is one instruction. Room and object numbers are as the game uses them (the reference pages list both).
In an instruction, 'plan dobj' and 'plan iobj' are not fixed objects: they mean the direct and indirect object currently held in the actor's plan registers (bytes +4 and +5 of its actor record), so the same step works on whatever the actor is dealing with - for the player, the objects of the command just typed. 'plan verb' is the verb register (+3), 'self' is the acting character, and '-' means none.
The endgame (EA25-$EAF5). Once PHIPPS HAS PLANS is proven (EB19) he keeps asking 'HAVE YOU FOUND WHERE THE SALE LOCATION IS.' whenever you are with him. As soon as THE SALE LOCATION IS OLD MILL ROAD is proven too (EB11) he sets off (EA5B) - with or without you: he signals Chief Constable Straker, travels to Leatherhead (EB72), climbs into the police cab (EA69) and has the constable drive to Old Mill Road. There, if you are not in the cab with him (EA78) he gives up and goes home (EA84). If you are, and Phipps is still there (EA7E), they drive back to Main Street and walk north to platform two at Leatherhead (room 55), and he says 'I AM AFRAID THAT BASIL HAS ELUDED US HOLMES.'. Left alone he waits there for a train and goes home on it (EAD0, then EA95 and EB28: platform four, Kings Cross Road, a cab to Parliament Street). But that waiting loop is the one stretch of this part of his script without the busy flag, so he takes orders there; and once off platform two with no train in, his script follows you (verb 134, EAE2) until you reach platform one at Victoria (room 30) - where Phipps is heading, and where Lestrade has to be for the winning ending (9E48). On platform two with his script at EAD0, SAY TO LESTRADE "FOLLOW ME" and he followed every step south to Main Street and beyond (carrying out the order - his script position moved to the order's own sub-script, E482 then DD5F). Either way, if he gets back to Scotland Yard he says that Basil has eluded you, and ends the game with the papers at 10am (EAB3).
The chase. The police cab holds only so much: climb in too early and you and Watson fill it, so Lestrade is left behind and the cab drives off without him; too late and Lestrade and Chief Constable Straker fill it ('I SEE NO POLICE CAB THAT YOU CAN CLIMB INTO'); in the minute Lestrade climbs in, you, Watson and Lestrade fit and Straker stays out. Then: Old Mill Road at 3:13pm, 'As you enter Basil Phipps whips his horses into action...' at 3:14, back in Main Street at 4:47, and Phipps walks to platform two and boards the train at 4:54. It leaves at 4:55 - one minute before Lestrade, still saying that Basil has eluded you, will take an order. Told to follow, he and Watson came on the next train (6:18), which reached platform four at 7:30; Phipps had left the game by 6:45 (between 6:30 and 6:45 in the logs), having found nobody at Victoria. Asking the constable to drive the police cab to Buckingham Palace Road did nothing; asked for Kings Cross Road - one of its three routes (9AA3) - it went, with Lestrade and Watson aboard, but took 95 minutes and arrived at 6:42, just as Phipps walked onto platform one at Victoria. The trains run every 90 minutes (F278), so missing the 4:55 costs an hour and a half. The plain GO TO and the train both lose; the furious police cab and the underground train win - see below.
The winning line. With suprising agility Watson pushes you out of the way of Basils first shot allowing Lestrade time to pull his gun and capture the culprits. As incredible as it may seem you have solved this case. You truly deserve the title of worlds greatest detective.' Two minutes to spare.
The order that matters on Wednesday: THE SALE LOCATION IS OLD MILL ROAD said at 10:53am; hansom cab (not Lestrade's) to Kings Cross Road; noon train; out at platform one; police cab at 1:37pm in the minute Lestrade boards; at 4:56pm on platform two SAY TO LESTRADE "FOLLOW ME" once; police cab GO FURIOUSLY TO KINGS CROSS ROAD (74 minutes); the 6:29 train from Kings Cross platform one, boarded in the minute it is in, so that Lestrade (who follows a turn behind) is aboard; Victoria platform one at 6:41.
Left alone on platform two at 4:55pm Lestrade says 'has eluded us Holmes' at 4:56 and takes the 6:15pm train (script EBF1), reaches platform four at 7:31, Kings Cross Road at 7:41, a hansom cab at 7:46, Parliament Street at 8:01 and Scotland Yard at 8:06pm (script EA99, which then waits for 10pm; EAAD puts him off stage at 10:01pm). The speech is only printed if you are in the room: riding the same train and cab and walking EAST into Scotland Yard at 8:02pm, you hear 'I am afraid that Basil has eluded us Holmes.' at 8:04pm. At Thursday 10:00am the script is at EAB3 and the papers ending fires (end7_eluded_at_scotland_yard).
