E39C: HL = the money a thing holds (+9/+10)
Unpacks IX+9/+10, seven bits a byte, keeping the sign.
Input
IX the thing
Output
HL total in pence
E39C LD L,(IX+$09) Read the adjective low byte (+9) of the thing record at IX into L
E39F LD H,(IX+$0A) Read the adjective high byte (+10) of the thing record at IX into H
E3A2 RL L Rotate L left through the carry - Put them back together: HL = H x 128 + L, H's sign kept by SRA
E3A4 SRA H Shift H right keeping its sign
E3A6 RR L Rotate L right through the carry
E3A8 RET Return to the caller
