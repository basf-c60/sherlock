CD95: Plan opcode 6, PRINT: print a message
The message address follows the opcode. Always succeeds.
Input
IX The instruction (+1/+2 the message)
Output
The step succeeds
CD95 LD L,(IX+$01) Read byte +1 of the record at IX into L - HL = the message
CD98 LD H,(IX+$02) Read byte +2 of the record at IX into H
CD9B CALL $A59F Print it
CD9E JP $CE8F Succeed
