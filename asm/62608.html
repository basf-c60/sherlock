F490: Fill the area around the pen in colour A
A scan-line flood fill: the colour goes to F60B, seeds are kept on the stack (a $00B0 marker ends them), and each row is filled left and right, seeding the rows above and below (F48E/$F48F).
How it works:
1. Keep the colour at F60B and put a marker on the stack.
2. From the seed, go left until the pixel is set or the edge is reached, colouring as it goes.
3. Work along the lines above and below: the first clear pixel of each run of clear pixels is pushed as a seed for later.
4. Pop seeds and repeat until the marker is reached.
Input
A The colour
DE The seed point
Output
The area around the seed filled; DE, HL kept
