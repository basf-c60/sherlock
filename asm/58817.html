E5C1: Action record: is the player in my room? (RUN at $E781)
Addresses named in the title: E781.
Compares the room of the first actor in the table (the player, 9860) with the current actor's and goes on to E5F0, which refuses if they differ (and if the actor is the direct object). It is used in Watson's script (actor 3, E72F, at E781).

$A017 The thing to compare with
Output
F From $E5F0: NZ if the player is in the same room
