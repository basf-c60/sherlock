9B50: A cabbie told where to go: can he get there from here?
Sets up the cab ($A017) for the journey - its +1 from the actor in $A036, its room (+15) and A00A from the cabbie's - and looks for a journey from here to the destination in $A034 (9C07). If there is one, returns Z. If not, the cabbie replies 'SORRY GUV'NOR I CANNOT GET THERE FROM 'ERE' (6C34) and this returns NZ to its caller's caller (it drops its own return address). Asked for Old Mill Road from Baker Street, 'The cabbie replies " Sorry guv'nor I cannot get there from'ere ".'
