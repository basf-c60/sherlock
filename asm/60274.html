EB72: Sub-script: Inspector Lestrade's journey to Leatherhead
Sub-script: RUN at E7E9; RUN at E98F; RUN at EA64; RUN at EF21.
A cab to Kings Cross Road (room 18), four steps north-east, a train to platform one (room 54), then verb 128 puts him in Leatherhead station (room 53).
Entry points: EB74 = body of record EB72.
Each sub-block is one instruction. Room and object numbers are as the game uses them (the reference pages list both).
In an instruction, 'plan dobj' and 'plan iobj' are not fixed objects: they mean the direct and indirect object currently held in the actor's plan registers (bytes +4 and +5 of its actor record), so the same step works on whatever the actor is dealing with - for the player, the objects of the command just typed. 'plan verb' is the verb register (+3), 'self' is the acting character, and '-' means none.
This journey collides with the player's own cab ride if the player is in the same hansom cab when Lestrade hails it (Tuesday about 12:20am and 7:05am, Wednesday after the sale statement): in the first trials Lestrade stayed in cab 33's off-stage room (pc EBC5) for the rest of the game. Hail the other cab, or take the train.
