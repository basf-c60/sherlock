D77E: Player's turn: parse the next sentence (RUN at $9932)
Addresses named in the title: 9932.
The body runs the sentence parser (AAB2) from the token at (D6BD). On success the rest of the line (9FF0) is kept at 9FF2 for next time and D6B8 cleared. On failure both word lists are freed (D87D, D88A) and - unless the parser's result was 2 and an action time was set - the game says 'WHAT ?' (6866) and fails. Part of the player's own plan script.
This record is one step of the loop in the player's own plan script at 9921: DO LOOK once, then read a line (D6BF), split it into words (D715), check there is a word (D769), parse a sentence (D77E), check there is a sentence (D7D9), turn it into a command (D7FA) and DO it. When you wear the china man's disguise the same script runs for him; the counts below include both.
