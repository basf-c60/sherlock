F1D6: Verb 136 (scripts only): is it hour n?
n counts 1-24. Succeeds only when the clock shows that hour: 13-24 are the pm hours, 1-12 the am ones.
Used by: internal verb 136 (IS HOUR: is the clock's hour (1-24) the direct object?).
Input
$A034 The hour, 1 to 24
Output
F NZ if the clock shows it; otherwise $DC15
F1D6 DEFB $07,$00 Action record header: flags $07; byte 1 $00
F1D8 LD A,($A034) n
F1DB LD B,$61 'a'...
F1DD CP $0D Compare A with $0D - ...unless n is 13 or more:
F1DF JR C,$F1E5 If A is below $0D, skip ahead to F1E5
F1E1 LD B,$70 'p'
F1E3 SUB $0C and 12 less
F1E5 LD HL,$9FE0 Point HL at the hour, 1-12 (9FE0) - Is that the hour?
F1E8 CP (HL) Compare A with the byte at the hour, 1-12 (9FE0)
F1E9 JP NZ,$DC15 No: refuse
F1EC LD A,($9FE2) Read 'a' or 'p' (9FE2) into A - And is it 'a' or 'p' as wanted?
F1EF CP B Compare A with B
F1F0 JP NZ,$DC15 No: refuse
F1F3 OR $01 Make A non-zero, which clears the zero flag, and hand that back to the caller - Yes: succeed
F1F5 RET Return to the caller
