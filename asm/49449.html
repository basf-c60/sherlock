C129: Narrow the text lines to the left half (a picture is showing)
Called at A284, after the 18 newlines that clear the text window and before the room's picture is drawn in the top right quarter (F3C2). Sets C128, the characters-left count (C1F5) to 18, the print position to $5020 and the pixel shift to 2, and clears the page line count ($A019). From then on the scroll (C316) moves only the left 16 bytes of each pixel line and leaves the colours alone, so the picture stays where it is while the text runs beside it.
In room 6 the description comes out 18 characters to a line ('You are in an | entrance hallway. | Above there is the | stairway...').
