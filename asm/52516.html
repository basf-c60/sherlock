CD24: Plan opcode 8, SIGNAL: leave a token for another actor
Allocates a 2-byte node (this actor, the argument) on the list headed at 9FE7. AWAIT (opcode 9) in the other actor's script looks for it. Always succeeds.
Input
IX The instruction (+1 the actor signalled)
Output
The token left on the list at $9FE7; the step succeeds
