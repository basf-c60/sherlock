D898: Read a line from the keyboard, with a time limit
The pre-check of the player's first script step (D6BF). Sets the limit ($A03B = 3,000 polls), switches quoted printing on while the line is typed, and runs the line editor (D8AD). If the player stops typing for too long the editor gives up, the step fails, and the player's script does WAIT HERE instead (9948) - so the clock and the other characters carry on while the player thinks.
Leaving the prompt with no key pressed gives '> You wait.' and the clock runs on from 8:02 to 8:05, then again to 8:10, with no command typed.

F Z if nothing was typed in time; NZ if a line was typed
