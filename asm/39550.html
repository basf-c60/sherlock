9A7E: Action record: default for verb 32 (HELLO)
The record is only its two header bytes: HELLO has no body of its own, and the code that follows at 9A80 is what runs. HELLO by itself prints 'HI.' and takes no time; SAY TO WATSON "HELLO" makes Watson answer 'Watson says " Good morning "'.
