D008: Load the current actor's plan registers
IY = the actor's record in the table at 9860. Copies the actor's saved plan registers into 9FD6 (the plan's verb) and 9FD7/9FD8 (its direct and indirect objects) - from the actor record's +3, +4 and +5, or, when the actor is inside a sub-script, from the call frame CFB6 finds (+6, +3 and +4). Script arguments $FA, $FD and $FC read these registers (see CEDE). D026 is the matching save.
