9E85: Game-over record: the Major is arrested
Does nothing once $9E88 - the operand of its own LD A,$00 (self-modifying) - is non-zero, which the routine of the case statement MAJOR PERCIVAL FFOULKES IS INNOCENT sets when it is proven (9A3D, which also prints 70F0: 'VERY GOOD HOLMES, I SHALL RETURN TO LEATHER HEAD TOMORROW TO HAVE ANOTHER LOOK AROUND.'). Otherwise prints 6975 ('AS THE MAJOR ENTERS LESTRADE ATTEMPTS TO ARREST HIM...') and ends the game with 69BF ('...WELL HOLMES THAT WRAPS UP THIS CASE, SORRY YOU WERE NOT OF MORE HELP...') if Lestrade is where the player is ($A009 = A00A), or 6A26 ('INSPECTOR LESTRADE ENTERS AND SAYS...') if not. Run by Lestrade's script (E96B).
Lestrade's script reaches this record at about 1am on Tuesday with $9E88 still 0, and the game ends with 'Inspector Lestrade enters and says to you " Well Holmes that wraps up this case, sorry you were not of more help but you cannot solve them all you know. ". Despite his assurances you cannot help feeling there should be more to this case. Perhaps you should try again.' (the player was elsewhere, so 6975 was not shown).
9E74 is the shared ending: prints the message in HL, waits for a key (C306), and restarts (B0AC).
