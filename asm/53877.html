D275: Is thing A carried by the actor, perhaps inside something? (carry = yes)
Follows thing A's container (+1) upwards (D288, through D237) until it reaches the actor in $A036 (carry set) or something not held by anything ($FF: carry clear). A = $FF or $FE (no thing, a number) also gives carry.
Input
A the thing
Output
F carry if the actor has it
