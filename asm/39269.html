9965: The case: statements about the crime, and the evidence each needs
Eleven groups. Each starts with a statement record: +0 a thing or person, +1 a relation number, +2 another thing; +3 flags; and, with bit 4 or 5 of the flags, +4/+5 a routine run when it is proven. Bit 2 of the flags opens a group of evidence records (+0 and +2 are things, +1 is 0) and bit 3 closes it; BE5A steps over one record and BE72 over a whole group. In the state bits, bit 0 means the statement is waiting to be proven and bit 1 that it has been (BCC9 sets bit 1 and runs the routine once the evidence checks out through BF08; 9A74 does the same from a routine).
With the relation words filled in (994D) the statements read as sentences: MAJOR PERCIVAL FFOULKES IS INNOCENT, MRS BROWN KILLED HERSELF, MRS JONES KILLED TRICIA FENDER, PHIPPS HAS PLANS, SALE LOCATION IS OLD MILL ROAD, TRICIA FENDER HAD PLANS, TRICIA FENDER BLACKMAILED MRS BROWN, and four with no evidence and no routine (flags $B4: bit 5 set) saying that Tricia Fender or Phipps killed one of the two victims. The order is as stored (first thing, verb, second thing), which is also the order the player's words are matched in (BD69). Some groups also hold a statement as evidence (MAJOR PERCIVAL FFOULKES VISITED OPIUM DEN, under the Major's innocence). An evidence record with relation 0 is a pair of things that the player must have seen (BE91: flags $00, both things' +6 bit 7) or examined (flags $40, +6 bit 6).
So nothing visible yet separates the bit-5 statements.
How a piece of evidence is checked: the top two bits of its flags choose the test. $00: both things seen (+6 bit 7); $40: both examined (+6 bit 6); $80: never true by itself - it only counts once something else has marked it done (bit 1), as the statement routines do through 9A74, so a nested statement such as MAJOR PERCIVAL FFOULKES VISITED OPIUM DEN ($8C) has to be established first; $C0: no test of its own, only its group. A record with children (bit 2) also needs all of them (BF08). Once a piece of evidence passes it is marked done and stays so.
With bit 1 of $9972 set first - as F103 does when the Major confesses - the opium-den statement is proven, the waiting innocence statement is proven with it ($9968 $A5 -> $A6), 9A3D sets $9E88 and Lestrade says 'Very good Holmes, I shall return to Leather head tomorrow to have another look around.'
