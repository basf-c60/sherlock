B0FD: Handlers for THATS, HE, IT and THEY
Pronouns: HE sets bit 7 and IT/THEY bit 6 of the current parse record's +$19 (used only when Watson or Lestrade weigh a statement: BD7E then takes the statement's subject from $9FF6, the last person a statement named, for HE, or from $9FFC, the last thing, for IT/THEY); the first-word versions (B135, B151) also mark the sentence as a statement to a character ($A024 = 2). THATS (B0FD) rearranges the parse record's object slots.
So pronouns are for statements, not commands.
Input
E Parse flags (bit 3: a clause has finished)
IY The sentence node
Output
E Bits 0 to 2, 6 and 7 set
IY The node that took the pronoun's words
