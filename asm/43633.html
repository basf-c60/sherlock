AA71: Shared action records, used through objects' own action lists
24 addresses of action records, ended by $0000. An object's own action list (see the entity records at 8CBA) maps a verb number to an index here; C960 looks the index up when the target object has an entry for the verb being carried out. That lets every door share one OPEN and one CLOSE, every chair one SIT IN, and so on.
Entry 5 is in fact TAKE OFF.
People have action lists too, after a 00 entry in their records (see C933): the local police man maps GO TO and GO FURIOUSLY TO to entries 1 and 2, the cabbies TAKE TO, GO TO and GO FURIOUSLY TO to 13, 14 and 15, Tricia Fender has entry 3 and Mrs Jones has entry 22 - which is how the entries no object uses are reached.
