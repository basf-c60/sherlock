DFB6: Action record: opening the wall safe on Monday (RUN at $DFAB)
Addresses named in the title: DFAB.
Part of the shared OPEN WALL SAFE script at DFAB. If the direct object is thing $59 (the wall safe) and the day is Monday, control passes to DFCC and Basil shoots you. Any other day it just succeeds.
Input
$A034 The direct object
Output
Continues at $E000, or at $DFCC on Monday for the wall safe

DFBD JP NZ,$E000 No: E000
DFC0 LD A,($9FE1) Read the day (9FE1) into A - And the day is Monday (0)?

DFC5 JP NZ,$E000 No: E000
