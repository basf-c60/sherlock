D113: Describe room A in full
The picture, if pictures are on and the room has one (A277); 'YOU ARE IN <thing>' when the actor is inside something (C863); the room's own text or 'YOU ARE ON/IN ... <name>' (D182); the exits (D60B); the things here (D68C); the people here (D4A7); end of line. To the north there is the front door.').
How it works:
1. Draw the room's picture if pictures are on and the room has one (A277).
2. If the actor is inside something (a cab, say), print 'YOU ARE IN' and how it is held (D5D3) and its name (D3D5).
3. Print the room's own description (D182), then list what can be seen.
Input
A A room number
HL The opening message
Output
The room described, with its picture if shown
