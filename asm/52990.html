CEFE: Skip past a plan instruction
CEFE also skips a success address (bit 7 set in A); CF03 skips only the opcode and operands: 1 byte for opcodes 10, 11 and 15, 2 for 1, 8, 9 and 13, 3 for 3, 6 and 14, 4 for 2 and 5, and 5 for 4, 7 and 12. The INC HL chain at CF3D-CF41 is entered at the point that gives the right count.
Input
A The opcode byte
HL The instruction
Output
HL Past the instruction (CF03 skips the opcode and operands only)
CEFE BIT 7,A Bit 7 of the opcode byte: a success address (2 bytes) follows...
CF00 CALL NZ,$CF40 ...skip it (enter the INC HL chain two from the end)
