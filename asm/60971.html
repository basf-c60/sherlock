EE2B: Plan script: Basil Phipps (actor 6) - the spy with the plans
Entry point: EE2B = phipps (actor 6).
Each sub-block is one instruction. Split off from the block before, which it merely follows in memory.
In an instruction, 'plan dobj' and 'plan iobj' are not fixed objects: they mean the direct and indirect object currently held in the actor's plan registers (bytes +4 and +5 of its actor record), so the same step works on whatever the actor is dealing with - for the player, the objects of the command just typed. 'plan verb' is the verb register (+3), 'self' is the acting character, and '-' means none.
At Old Mill Road (EF26) he waits for 3pm, then starts a countdown of 16 minutes plus a random number (EF34). Each minute he checks whether you are at Old Mill Road (EF39). If you arrive in time, 'AS YOU ENTER HE WHIPS HIS HORSES INTO ACTION AND SPEEDS OFF DOWN THE ROAD.' (EF47, message 7573): he is put in Leatherhead's Main Street, waits for 4pm and 48 minutes more, walks to the station, takes a train to platform four in London, walks five steps south-west to Kings Cross Road, takes a cab to Buckingham Palace Road and walks east twice, to platform one at Victoria (room 30). If you are there (EF83) he runs 9E48 - the last scene; if not, he leaves the game (EF8C).
With Watson and Lestrade beside you: 'Basil Phipps enters. ... As Basil enters he sees you and pulls a gun. With suprising agility Watson pushes you out of the way of Basils first shot allowing Lestrade time to pull his gun and capture the culprits. As incredible as it may seem you have solved this case. You truly deserve the title of worlds greatest detective.' Without Lestrade, or without Watson: '...With his usual skill his first shot disposes of you. Perhaps you should try again.' With you one room away, he walked in and left the game.
The flight from EF4A on is shared with the German agent (EFF6), who waits at Old Mill Road too.
Phipps is in the bedroom (room 35) through the night, in the hall (33) at 10:06, in Camden Street (32) at 10:09 and in his cab (room 1) at 10:15 - the same whether the player is in room 102 or in the street. At 9:50am he shoots whoever is in the bedroom, in disguise or not.
Why Phipps's Wednesday morning differs between logs: at EEE9 his script tests whether you are in Camden Street or Basil's front yard, or the china man is in either. If so he plays the messenger scene (EFAC) and waits 15 minutes before leaving the bedroom; if not he goes at once. With the player indoors he is in Camden Street at 9:55am; with the player in the front yard, as in the winning route, about 10:06-10:10.
