CDBF: Plan opcode 4, ASK: order another character to do something
Verb 46 (TALK TO) with the person as the direct object; queues an 8-byte node on the list at 9FEE (person, $FF, verb, dobj, iobj) and prints 682A, which quotes the order: the cabbie is told 'HAIL THE HANSOM CAB' this way, for example. The person carries the order out on its next turn (CCA7).
With the player moved to Inspector Lestrade at the start, his script hails a hansom cab, climbs in, and at 8:04 runs ASK (bytes 44 FA 1D FC FF: the person is the plan's remembered thing, here the cabbie; verb 29 GO TO; the plan's indirect object, a room); CE10 then prints 'Inspector Lestrade remarks to the cabbie " Go to Kings Cross Road. "'.
Input
IX The instruction (+1 the person, +2 verb, +3 and +4 objects)
Output
The order queued at $9FEE; the step succeeds
