9ED1: After-turn routine: the gardener
See 9EC1.
At 9pm he is put off stage with the state word SLEEPING and +5 bit 6 cleared (the bit the flag-word table at C471 calls DEAD when it is clear); at his next turn he is put back in the room he left and woken.
Input
IY The actor's entry in the actor table
Output
F None used
9ED1 LD HL,$9ED0 The gardener's state byte 9ED0: the room he left, or 0 while about
This entry point is used by the routine at 9ECB.
9ED4 LD BC,$0000 Load BC with $0000 - B = 0, C = 0 (the cook joins here too; Strachan comes in at 9ED7 with B = 74, C = $80)
This entry point is used by the routine at 9EC1.
