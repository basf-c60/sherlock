B86A: Hand quoted words to the person spoken to (SAY TO / TALK TO)
Called by the TALK TO body (DDAE) with A = 1 if the person has a plan script (an actor, CC0E) and 0 if not. Takes the next quoted sentence off the list at 9FEC. For an actor, B8AA puts it on the order/remark list (9FEE) as an 8-byte node: +0 the person, +1 0, +5/+6 the words, +7 = $A024 (2 for a statement); the person deals with it on its next turn (B936). For anyone else the words are just used up.
How it works:
1. Take the next quoted sentence from the list at 9FEC and unlink it.
2. If the person has a plan script (A = 1), parse the words as a sentence of their own (B8AA) and put them on the order and remark list at 9FEE as an 8-byte node.
3. Otherwise skip the words to the end of the line and mark it with a $90 token.
Input
A 1 if the person spoken to has a plan script; 0 if not
Output
The quoted sentence handed over or used up
