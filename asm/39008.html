9860: The actor table: who acts, and where each is in its plan script
24 eight-byte records, ended by $FF, walked once per turn. +0 the actor's thing id; +1/+2 its position in its plan script (see CC78); +3 the plan's verb, +4/+5 the plan's direct and indirect objects ($FF = none; loaded by D008, saved by D026); +6/+7 the minute (counted like 9FDD from the start) at which the actor next acts, set by the main loop (A064); 0 at the start, so everyone acts in the first minute. CC0E finds a record by id. The seven steam trains and two cabbies are actors too.
