BB67: Lestrade's reply to something the player says
Parses the remark into a statement the same way as Watson's reply (BA67) and checks it against the case table: a statement whose evidence is complete is proven (BCC9) and Lestrade says so; otherwise it is marked as waiting and he says he does not believe the player has the evidence (72A6, 72BD).
A plain HELLO does not come here at all: Lestrade answers 'Good afternoon' through 9A80.
the player put in Lestrade's room, then remarks typed): BASIL KILLED - a statement that stops after the verb - goes to BBBE and Lestrade says 'What' (7243). After MAJOR FFOULKES IS INNOCENT is left waiting the same remark proves it and Lestrade says 'Very good Holmes, I shall return to Leather head tomorrow...', through BBE5.
BBFB-BC17, which names a statement with flags bit 4 that is still waiting. Only the four flags-$B4 statements (Tricia Fender or Phipps killed Mrs Brown or Mrs Jones) have that bit, and each has a child record that asks only that thing 0, the player, has been seen.

IY The remark's sentence node
A 0
Output
A reply printed
