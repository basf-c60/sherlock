D0D0: Put word HL into the 'YOU ARE ...' message
Writes it, as a word token (bit 7 set), at $68DE.
Input
HL A word reference
Output
The word written into the YOU ARE message at $68DE
DE Kept
D0D0 PUSH DE Save DE on the stack - Store word HL, with bit 7 of its high byte set, big-endian at $68DE in the YOU ARE message
D0D1 LD DE,$68DE Point DE at $68DE in "Message: "YOU ARE IN""
D0D4 EX DE,HL Swap DE and HL
D0D5 SET 7,D Set bit 7 of D
D0D7 LD (HL),D Store D at the address HL points to
D0D8 INC HL Step HL on to the next byte
D0D9 LD (HL),E Store E at the address HL points to
D0DA POP DE Restore DE from the stack
D0DB RET Return to the caller
