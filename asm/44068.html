AC24: Read a noun phrase into the pattern at $AAA8
Addresses named in the title: $AAA8.
Clears ten bytes at $AAA8, then takes tokens: an article ($80) once; up to three of class $10 or $70 (prepositions; counted in $AAA7, stored by AC8F); adjectives ($60, by AC94); and ends on the noun ($50) or a number ($F0), whose reference goes to $AAAC. Carry when a word comes in the wrong place; on return NZ if bit 5 of E is set.
How it works:
1. Clear the ten bytes of the noun-phrase pattern at $AAA8 and set E bits 2, 4 and 5.
2. Take tokens. An article ($80) is allowed once, at the start; a second one returns carry.
3. Up to three words of class $10 or $70 (adjectives and prepositions) are counted in $AAA7 and stored by AC8F or AC94.
4. A noun ends the phrase. The pattern is then copied into the node as the first or second object (ACA6).
Input
E Flags for the kinds of word still allowed (bits 2, 4, 5)
Output
F Carry set for a word out of place
E Updated
