BF79: Print the clock: day, hour, minute, a or p
Builds 'Mon 08:02am' in BF6E from the day (9FE1), the hour (9FE0), the minute (9FDF) and the letter (9FE2), and prints it in the status bar (C1A8).
How it works:
1. Write the three-letter day name into the buffer at BF6E, choosing it from the table at BF59 by the day number (9FE1).
2. Write the hour (9FE0) and the minute (9FDF) as two digits each, found by repeated subtraction of ten (BFBB), with the space and colon already in the buffer.
3. Write the letter a or p (9FE2) and print the eleven characters, for example 'Mon 08:02am', in the status bar (C1A8).
