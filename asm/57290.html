DFCA: Action record: Basil shoots you (RUN at $EF99, and from $DFB6)
Addresses named in the title: EF99, DFB6.
Game over. If Basil (thing 6, Phipps) is not in the player's room it prints 'Basil enters and says " Holmes, your interference is becoming annoying. " ...' (759C), if he is it prints the version without 'enters' (75D2), then ends the game through 9E74. Reached from the wall-safe script on Monday and from Phipps's wake-up routine EFC7 when you are inside his house at that time. The three instructions at DFE3-DFE8 belong to the pre-check of the next record (DFEA), not to this one.
