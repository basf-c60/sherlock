AAB2: Parse the next sentence from the word list
Called for the player's typed line ($D78F, part of the player's script step D77E) and for the words of a remark ($B8BF). Sorts tokens into the parse record (IY) - the verb and its particles (AB91), objects (AC24) - and dispatches on punctuation: a full stop finishes the sentence (AD57), a comma starts another object or sentence (ACCC), a special word (class $90) goes to AFB3. NZ if it does not make sense (AF67).
How it works:
1. Clear the sentence count and start with every kind of word allowed (E = $FF).
2. Decide the sentence's mode ($A024). If a line of sentences is still being worked through it is an ordinary command (mode 1). Otherwise try to read a noun phrase first (AC24): a sentence that opens with one and has no preposition is a statement such as MRS JONES KILLED TRICIA FENDER (mode 2, subject kept in the parse node); anything else is a command (mode 1) and reading starts again from the beginning.
3. Read the verb and its particles (AB91). Carry means the words make no sense.
4. A special word (token class $90) goes to AFB3. A comma ($A0) starts another sentence (ACCC).
5. Read the objects: each noun phrase (AC24) is filed in the parse node (ACA6), and this repeats as long as noun phrases follow.
6. A full stop ($B0) completes the sentence (AD57). In a statement, any other word must be a relation word (ABF3), which ends the subject and carries on with the object.
7. Anything else makes no sense: return NZ (AF67).
Input
IY The parse node for the sentence
