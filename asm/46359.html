B517: ALL BUT: is the candidate one of the things after BUT?
Only when $A01C is set (bit 7 of the verb's first word reference, copied from B39C at $B1ED): walks the parse nodes after IY (AEA9) and, in any marked with bit 6 of +1 - the node B029 opens when BUT (or EXCEPT, a synonym) follows ALL - looks for things the words at +8 name (D36A); NZ if the candidate in $A034 is one of them, and B4FD then passes over it.
Input
$A034 The candidate
Output
F NZ if the candidate is one of the BUT things; Z if not
