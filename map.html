Map and rooms

Decoded from the room table at 840B. The map is a grid: a room that is north of another is drawn above it, one to the east is drawn to its right, and so on, area by area. Arrows are the exits the room table gives; a two-headed arrow is an exit that exists in both directions; a red dashed line is an exit that is blocked for the player by something too narrow. An orange dashed line is an exit that cannot be drawn between neighbouring cells because the exit table is not a flat plan (the garden maze does this, and so do a couple of single exits in Brown's house and Basil's London house); its directions are written on it, for example NW/SE for "northwest one way, southeast back". A small purple tag under a room, such as S→76, is an exit to another area or another floor. Click a room to jump to its row below. Rooms with no walking exits are vehicles (the cab and trains), reached by getting in rather than by walking.

Most rooms have none and are described from their name and exits alone.
